# twister-color-pumpkinpipe
A little game/project that uses MediaPipe and OpenCV simplified from the Pumpkinpipe library.

How to play:
- On the top of the webcam will have a symbol of a hand sign and a colored circle. These are information for your left and right hands.
- You will see your camera divided into 4 quadrants. In the center, each quadrant is marked with Red, Blue, Green, and Yellow.
- With the information above, you need to place your hands in the specified colored quadrants and your finger positioning matches the hand sign symbols.
- The program checks every frame if your hands match the instructions. If the computer verifies hand and finger positioning, the next set immediately begins automatically.
- Your score increases along with a score multiplier for every round successful.
- Each round has a timer, failing to meet the quota results in a loss round and you lose points and your multiplier is decreased.
- There is a Easy, Normal, and Hard mode and the user can configure these settings prior to the game starting.
- There is no main goal and its just a fun little game where you get lots of score.
